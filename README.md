# Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis
**Analyzing Booking Patterns and Cancellation Drivers in Portuguese Hotels**

---

## Project Overview

**Author:** Naomi Nguyen
**Date:** January 2026  
**Tools:** Python | SQL | Power BI  

### The Challenge
Two hotels in Portugal, a city property and a resort location, are experiencing high booking cancellation rates that negatively affect revenue predictability and operational efficiency. This project analyzes over 100,000 hotel reservations to identify the root causes of cancellations and propose actionable strategies to reduce them.

### Project Goals
- Analyze booking and cancellation performance
- Identify high-risk customer segments and booking behaviors
- Quantify revenue loss caused by cancellations
- Propose data-driven strategies to reduce future cancellations

---

## Dataset Information

**Source:** Kaggle Hotel Booking Demand Dataset  
**Period:** July 2015 to August 2017  
**Records:** 100,000+ hotel reservations  
**Scope:** Two hotels in Portugal  
- City Hotel (Lisbon)  
- Resort Hotel (Algarve)

## Key Findings

### Overall Performance

| Metric | Value |
|------|------|
| Total Bookings | 86,113 |
| Confirmed Bookings | 62,109 (72%) |
| Cancelled Bookings | 24,004 (28%) |
| Total Revenue | $34.41M |
| Revenue Lost to Cancellations | $11.48M (33.4%) |

---

### Critical Insight
95.8 percent of cancellations originated from bookings without deposit requirements.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

1. Introduce deposits for early bookings  
2. Strengthen cancellation policies  
3. Build guest loyalty programs  
4. Promote direct booking channels  
5. Recover revenue from last-minute cancellations  

---

## Business Impact

- Target cancellation rate: 20–22 percent  
- Estimated revenue recovery: $2–3M annually  
- Improved occupancy forecasting  
- Reduced dependency on commission-heavy channels
